# Claude / Quad Handoff — Rebuild the Saloon Counter

## Main task

The current More Drinks counter looks poor and is not acceptable as the finished design. Rebuild the counter so it looks intentional, polished, attractive, and professionally integrated into Gus's Stardrop Saloon.

Do not simply defend, preserve, or lightly touch up the current counter. Treat this as a genuine redesign. Use your own design judgment and expertise to decide what the strongest result should look like; this handoff deliberately describes the desired outcome rather than prescribing construction steps.

## References to study

Before redesigning it, inspect the existing BentleyPlayz projects—especially **Bentley's Fast Food**—and use their stronger interior presentation, counter design quality, visual consistency, and overall polish as references. Also review the relevant assets and research already present in this repository. Adapt what works to the Stardrop Saloon instead of blindly copying another building.

## Quality expectations

- The counter must look like it belongs in the Stardrop Saloon and in Stardew Valley.
- It should read clearly as a purposeful, usable shop counter and look coherent with the surrounding room.
- Pay attention to proportions, alignment, tile-level detail, palette, visual hierarchy, and how the counter sits within the interior.
- The finished result should be a substantial improvement over the current version, not a cosmetic patch.
- Preserve the existing vanilla Saloon experience: Gus's normal stock, shop behavior, NPC interactions, and unrelated schedules must not be broken.
- Keep Android compatibility in mind, since the project is tested on Android.

## Project context

This is BentleyPlayz More Drinks, a Stardew Valley 1.6.15.3 / SMAPI 4.3.2.5 project. Its broader goal is to add 50 coffees and 50 teas and integrate them into Gus's Stardrop Saloon without removing vanilla content. The README contains the current project summary.

## Deliverable

Implement the redesigned counter in the project, review it in context with the surrounding Saloon interior, and make any adjustments needed for it to look finished. Do not stop at a mockup or a written proposal. Report what changed and identify any Android testing still needed.
